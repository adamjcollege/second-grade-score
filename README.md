# Turbo Score

Step-by-step 2nd grade practice, with 3rd grade "All-Star" challenges, starring Turbo the fluffy pup and Brix the brick buddy. Aligned to the North Carolina Standard Course of Study for grades 2 and 3.

- **Math Stadium:** Homework Helper, Fact Fast Break (+ − × ÷ with Streak, Three Strikes, and optional timed modes), place value to 1,000, skip-counting, comparing, 10 and 100 more or less, odd and even, adding in place-value columns, subtracting with regrouping, word problems, arrays, sharing, measuring, clocks, money, graphs, shapes, fractions, and area and perimeter.
- **Word Speedway:** stories, poems, plays, nonfiction, and a five-chapter mystery with "Prove it!" evidence questions; a Listening Lab; Homophone Hustle and Homophone Hunt (their, there, they're and friends); word choice; word meaning; Spelling Shootout (read-aloud dictation); capitals; commas; apostrophes; grammar; print practice; and a Paragraph Builder that flags homophone mix-ups.
- **Locker Room:** dress up your own fluffy pup (fur, spots, ears, jersey and number, hats, sports gear, and extras) and fill a trophy case. Stars unlock gear and are never spent.
- **For grown-ups:** settings (including hiding the 3rd grade All-Star levels), how it works, and a standards coverage table.

On phones, double-tap zoom is off, the Back button returns to the home screen, the screen stays awake during games, and it can be added to the home screen as a full-screen app.

It's a static site with no build step: open `index.html`, or publish with GitHub Pages (main branch, root folder). Progress is saved in the browser on each device.
